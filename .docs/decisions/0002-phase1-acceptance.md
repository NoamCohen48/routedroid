# 0002 — Phase 1 acceptance

Status: closed, 2026-09-21. Phase 2 may begin.

Phase 1 (implementation-plan §4) delivered:

- `protocol/version-1.md` with golden fixtures (`protocol/fixtures/`, generated by
  `protocol/tools/gen-fixtures.py`) that `host/routedroid-proto` (Rust) and
  `android/protocol` (Kotlin) both pass.
- `host/routedroid`: `devices`, `start` (helper session, reverse mapping, record over adb
  stdin, launch, accept, session machine with keepalive, exact cleanup); fault taxonomy with
  exit codes; USB/emulator serials only.
- `android/app` (`dev.routedroid`): foreground `VpnService`, consent flow, rate-limited
  bootstrap activity, DUMP + shell-UID provider, protected transport, config validation,
  bounded pumps, status/stop UI.

Acceptance evidence: `integration-tests/phase1/README.md` (emulator Android 14 and Samsung
Android 10 over USB, positive and negative cases, hostile app).

Decisions taken in this phase:

- ERROR is host → app only; the app reports everything, including violations it detects,
  through VPN_ERROR (spec §4.6).
- The app answers PING and also pings on its own after 10 s of silence and closes after
  30 s without a frame; the host does the same.
- `emulator-5554`-style serials are accepted (transport class `Emulator`) so the emulator
  can be used for tests; network serials remain refused (decision 0001, deferred gate 5).
- `--phone-ip` is manual in Phase 1; DHCP comes in Phase 3.
- Host deadlines before Active: 15 s from connect to Configuring (anyone on the phone can
  connect to the reverse port), 120 s for the VPN consent; expiry is ERROR `protocol_error`
  and full cleanup.
- Not enforced: §7.1 "at most one record per 60 s" on the provider (only the shell UID can
  write; a new record replaces the old one). Revisit if the bootstrap surface widens.
